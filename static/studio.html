Interview guide

How will people take part?

For interviews, the device speaks Muse's questions and listens to answers of up to 90 seconds. A quiet pause can end a recording; Muse Voice transcribes it. Participants review the transcript before sending their answer. They can pause voice mode or type instead. Audio goes to Meta and is not saved by this app. There is no video avatar.

Survey answers, interview text and confirmed transcripts go to Muse Spark 1.3 for follow-ups and analysis. Responses are saved in this computer's unencrypted local database. This prototype has no researcher sign-in.
